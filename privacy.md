# Privacy Policy for Automated Post Uploader

**Effective Date:** August 22, 2026

At **Automated Post Uploader** ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application interacts with your data.

## 1. Information We Access
Our application is designed solely to automate posting content to your designated Facebook Page. To achieve this, the app may access:
* Your basic public profile information (such as your name and user ID) to verify account ownership.
* The list of Facebook Pages you manage (`pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`).
* Permission to create and publish posts on your managed pages (`pages_manage_posts`).

## 2. How We Use Information
The data and permissions accessed by our application are strictly used for:
* Authenticating your connection to the Facebook Graph API.
* Executing automated post uploads to your Facebook Page as requested by you.

We **do not** store, sell, rent, or share your personal data, tokens, or page information with any third parties. All operations are executed locally or through secure official Facebook APIs.

## 3. Data Security
We implement appropriate technical measures to ensure that your access tokens and interactions with the Facebook Graph API remain secure. 

## 4. User Control & Data Deletion
Since we do not store your personal information on external servers, you can revoke our application's access at any time directly through your Facebook account settings:
* Go to your Facebook Account **Settings & Privacy** > **Settings** > **Apps and Websites**.
* Locate **Automated Post Uploader** and click **Remove**.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated on this page with a revised effective date.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your development contact channel.
